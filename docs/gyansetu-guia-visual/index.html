The key idea

One shared student record, several operational roles

GyanSetu is primarily a staff-operated information system. Administrators, Support Staff, and Teachers contribute different information to the same student record.

Administrator

Configures the organisation, manages access, and supervises the complete system.

Support Staff

Maintains student data and keeps day-to-day programme operations accurate.

Student record

Stores personal, family, enrolment, centre, attendance, and programme information. It is not a student-facing account flow.

Current operating model: students do not sign in or use GyanSetu directly. The system is used by staff to manage their records and programme participation.

Daily workflow

Teacher · Attendance and student follow-up

Staff Dashboard → assigned centre/group → attendance → follow-up

Start of the day

  1. Review assigned groups and notices.
  2. Open the relevant centre or group.
  3. Take attendance.
  4. Save and confirm that the register is complete.

Student follow-up

  1. Open the permitted student profile.
  2. Review attendance history.
  3. Check relevant personal or programme information.
  4. Record or communicate concerns through the agreed process.

What Teachers use

  • Staff Dashboard.
  • Assigned centre/group lists.
  • Attendance registration.
  • Permitted student profiles.
  • Relevant operational reports.

What is not part of the current flow

  • Lesson Planner.
  • Student homework or submissions.
  • Student-facing resources.
  • Student login and self-service.
  • Formal assessment workflow.
Teacher boundary: Teachers mainly work with their assigned groups. GyanSetu hides the Admin menu category for the Teacher role and restricts attendance group selection.
Possible future improvement: a lightweight assessment process may be useful, but it is not currently part of the operational workflow and would require a separate design decision.
Teacher3
System governance

Administrator · Configuration and control

Structure → users → enrolment → permissions → monitoring → rollover

Prepare the system

  • School years and periods.
  • Levels and centres/groups.
  • Classes and organisational structure.
  • Attendance codes and settings.

Manage staff access

  • Create staff accounts.
  • Assign roles and permissions.
  • Enable or block login access.
  • Assign Teachers and responsible staff.

Admit a student

  1. Review the application or new record.
  2. Validate the information.
  3. Create or update the student record.
  4. Enrol in the current year, level, and centre/group.

Monitor operations

  • Attendance and missing registers.
  • Student data quality.
  • Users and permissions.
  • Imports, exports, and reports.
End of cycle: create or activate the new school year, roll students forward, update levels and centres, mark transitions or exits, and verify staff assignments.

Operations and data quality

Support Staff · The operational bridge

Application → verification → enrolment → data checks → follow-up

Applications and records

  • Review incoming information.
  • Correct personal details.
  • Validate guardians and contacts.
  • Complete programme-specific fields.

Enrolment

  • Confirm the current school year.
  • Assign the correct level.
  • Assign the centre/group.
  • Verify that the student appears in staff lists.

Follow-up

  • Find incomplete attendance registers.
  • Review prolonged absences.
  • Support student welfare processes.
  • Keep records up to date.

Reporting

  • Student lists.
  • Demographic and attendance data.
  • Operational exports.
  • Data-quality checks and queries.
Permissions matter: Support Staff share the staff dashboard foundation, but not every Support Staff account has the same menu. Visible actions depend on permissions assigned by an Administrator.

Information held

  • Personal and family information.
  • Guardians and contact details.
  • Current school year enrolment.
  • Level and centre/group.
  • Attendance and relevant support data.
  • Transition or leaving information.

Explicitly not used

  • Student authentication.
  • Student Dashboard.
  • Timetable and Planner access.
  • Lessons and resources.
  • Homework and deadlines.
  • Own results, notices, or reports.
Important: creating a student user record is not the same as enrolling the student. For operational visibility, the record must be linked to the current school year, a level, and a centre/group.

How to describe this accurately

The student record is the shared operational record that connects admissions, enrolment, attendance, support, reporting, progression, and exit. Students themselves do not interact with the system.

Quick diagnosis when something is missing

1 Is the correct staff role active?
2 Does the role have permission?
3 Is the current school year selected?
4 Is the student enrolled in that year?
5 Is the level correct?
6 Is the centre/group correct?
7 Is the Teacher assigned to that group?
8 Is the staff account active?
Future consideration—not current practice: GyanSetu contains assessment-related modules inherited from Gibbon. Before enabling them, define the educational purpose, minimum data set, responsible role, reporting need, and training required.
Development shortcut: trace each feature through active role and permission → menu action → page/process → database record.
